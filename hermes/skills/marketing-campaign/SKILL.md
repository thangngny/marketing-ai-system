# Marketing Campaign Skill

Turn an objective into a measurable multi-channel campaign draft. It never launches ads, commits budget, publishes, or sends outreach.

## When to Use

Use for campaign briefs, work plans, channel coordination, budget proposals, and measurement plans.

## Prerequisites

Use `marketing_handle_request` and `marketing_request_execution` for any proposed external action.

## How to Run

Define objective, ICP, insight, offer, message, CTA, assets, funnel, handoff, and KPIs.

## Quick Reference

Budget is a proposal, never authorization to spend.

## Procedure

1. Design a small test with baseline/control where possible.
2. Create channel and asset checklist.
3. Define MQL/SQL and Sales handoff.
4. Add tracking and decision thresholds.
5. List every approval gate.

## Pitfalls

Never launch or modify a campaign or budget from Phase 1.

## Verification

The plan says `DRAFT — NOT LAUNCHED` and identifies required credentials and approvals.
