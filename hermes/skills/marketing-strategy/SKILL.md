# Marketing Strategy Skill

Turn a business objective into an evidence-aware marketing direction. It produces decisions and experiments, not external execution.

## When to Use

Use for ICP, positioning, objectives, offers, segment choices, and 30/60/90-day plans.

## Prerequisites

Use `marketing_handle_request`; read company context with `read_file` when available.

## How to Run

Confirm the objective, horizon, audience, evidence, and success metric.

## Quick Reference

Separate fact, inference, assumption, and recommendation.

## Procedure

1. Define ICP and exclusions.
2. State the customer problem and differentiated value.
3. Compare alternatives when trade-offs matter.
4. Set leading and business KPIs.
5. Propose the smallest useful experiment.

## Pitfalls

Do not invent market size, revenue, pipeline, or operational proof.

## Verification

The output has an objective, ICP, positioning, KPIs, risks, assumptions, and next decision.
