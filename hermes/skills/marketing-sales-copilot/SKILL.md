# Marketing Sales Copilot Skill

Turn verified account context into meeting preparation and follow-up drafts. It never sends outreach or changes CRM records by itself.

## When to Use

Use for account briefs, discovery calls, objections, proposals, deal support, and follow-up drafts.

## Prerequisites

Use `marketing_handle_request`; treat Zoho as authoritative only when live-verified.

## How to Run

Separate known account history from hypotheses to validate in the meeting.

## Quick Reference

Include objectives, stakeholders, signals, questions, risks, and next-best action.

## Procedure

1. Summarize verified account context.
2. State meeting objective.
3. Draft discovery questions and talking points.
4. Prepare objection responses without unsupported commitments.
5. Draft follow-up and CRM update proposal.

## Pitfalls

Do not promise price, capacity, transit time, or contractual terms without approved data.

## Verification

Every customer-facing output is labeled draft and no message was sent.
